CIA Senior: Driving Operational Excellence

Better Team Results and Happier Clients

My leadership philosophy at Geek Squad is built on the pillars of leading by example, active coaching, and dedicated mentorship. By personally modeling technical excellence and fostering a culture of continuous learning, I drove tangible gains in department performance and improved key operational metrics.

Leading a Turnaround at Geek Squad

Situation

As a Counter Intelligence Agent Senior at Geek Squad, I inherited a team struggling with long repair times, inconsistent client experience, and low utilization. Department metrics showed frequent rework, missed deadlines, and frustrated clients, putting our reputation and revenue at risk.

Tools

I relied on hands-on technical expertise, Geek Squad diagnostic tools, ticketing and reporting systems, and one-on-one coaching sessions. I also used weekly standups, shadowing, and peer reviews to spot patterns and share best practices across the team.

Action

I led by example at the counter, modeling clear communication with clients and disciplined troubleshooting. I created simple playbooks for common issues, paired newer agents with experienced mentors, and used data from our systems to focus coaching on the highest-impact skills. I also set clear expectations and celebrated small wins to rebuild confidence.

Results

The changes I led improved the client experience, strengthened key performance metrics, reduced repair turn-time, and raised overall team morale. Clients saw clearer communication and more reliable service, while the team felt more confident, supported, and proud of the work we delivered
